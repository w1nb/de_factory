Factory - Bomb/Defuse (5 vs 5)

Converted from the local Minecraft world de_factory.
Scale: one block = 44 GoldSrc units.

Terrorists: Start at the five rooftop bed positions.
Plant the C4 at either former beacon location.

Counter-Terrorists: Start at the five front-gate bed positions.
Prevent the bombing or defuse the planted C4.

A: Western production area.
B: Eastern production area.

All iron doors are permanently shut and solid. Wooden doors are removed.
Both teams have buy zones at their spawn area.
Windows are unbreakable. The original ladder is climbable.
Railings have narrow gaps and solid player collision, including crouching.

Original game textures are embedded in the BSP.
The Minecraft world is preserved separately and unchanged.
